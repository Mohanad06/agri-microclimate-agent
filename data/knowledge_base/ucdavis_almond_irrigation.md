# UC Davis Extension: Almond Irrigation & Deficit Scheduling
Source: University of California Agriculture and Natural Resources (UC ANR Publication 8503)
URL: https://anrcatalog.ucanr.edu/pdf/8503.pdf
Crop: Almond
Category: Irrigation, Water Stress, Deficit Irrigation, Midday Stem Water Potential

## Midday Stem Water Potential (SWP) Guidelines
Midday stem water potential (SWP) is the most accurate diagnostic indicator of almond tree water stress. It measures the tension (negative pressure) in megapascals (MPa).
- Fully watered / No stress: -0.6 to -1.0 MPa. The tree is fully hydrated. Ideal during early vegetative growth.
- Mild stress: -1.0 to -1.4 MPa. Mild stress that does not restrict carbon assimilation or photosynthesis. This is the target stress level during hull split to control hull rot.
- Moderate stress: -1.4 to -1.8 MPa. Induces stomatal closure, reducing tree water loss and slowing shoot growth. Acceptable for post-harvest management.
- Severe stress: Below -1.8 MPa. Causes leaf yellowing, premature defoliation, and reduces flower bud differentiation for the subsequent season. Immediate irrigation required.

## Hull Split Deficit Irrigation Management
Regulated Deficit Irrigation (RDI) is implemented during the hull split stage to promote uniform ripening and reduce hull rot infection.
- Hull split timing: Typically occurs during July and August in California microclimates.
- Deficit strategy: Irrigation is managed to maintain stem water potential (SWP) between -1.4 and -1.8 MPa for 2 to 3 weeks prior to harvest.
- Caution: Deficit irrigation must not allow SWP to drop below -2.0 MPa, as this causes significant yield reduction and long-term tree decline.

## Soil Moisture and Ambient Temperature Interactions
High ambient heat index values accelerate soil moisture depletion, increasing water potential tension.
- Temperature interaction: Ambient temperatures exceeding 38°C (100°F) trigger rapid stomatal closure and tree stress, even in fully irrigated soils.
- Root-zone soil wetness proxy: Root-zone soil moisture (GWETROOT) must be maintained above 0.35 during vegetative growth and should not fall below 0.20 during RDI hull split.
