# Texas A&M AgriLife Extension: Cotton Thermal Limits & Heat Stress Guide
Source: Texas A&M AgriLife Extension Service (E-319: "Cotton Heat Stress Management")
URL: https://agrilifelearn.tamu.edu/s/
Crop: Cotton
Category: Temperature, Heat Stress, Flowering, Bolls

## Germination and Planting Limits
Cotton is a warm-season crop requiring warm soil for planting.
- Minimum soil temperature threshold: 15.5°C (60°F) at a 10 cm depth for 3 consecutive days before planting.
- Optimum temperature range: 25°C to 32°C (77°F to 90°F) for rapid seedling establishment.

## Flowering and Square Shedding Heat Limits
Cotton is sensitive to high night and day temperatures during flowering (squaring and bloom).
- Daytime temperature threshold: Daytime temperatures exceeding 36°C (97°F) reduce pollen viability and cause young square/boll shedding.
- Extreme heat limit: Temperatures exceeding 38°C (100°F) severely reduce lint yield and boll retention.
- Nighttime limit: Night temperatures above 26°C (79°F) restrict carbohydrate accumulation and accelerate fruit shedding.

## Soil Moisture Requirements
- Root-zone wetness threshold: Soil wetness should be maintained above 0.35 during peak boll filling.
