# UC Davis Extension: Grapevine Microclimate & Thermal Limits Guide
Source: University of California Agriculture and Natural Resources (UC ANR Publication 8622)
URL: https://anrcatalog.ucanr.edu/pdf/8622.pdf
Crop: Grape
Category: Temperature, Heat Stress, Flowering, Viticulture

## Germination and Budbreak Thresholds
Grapevine budbreak depends closely on spring temperature accumulation.
- Minimum air temperature threshold: Budbreak begins when temperatures consistently exceed 10°C (50°F).
- Optimum growth temperature range: 20°C to 30°C (68°F to 86°F) promotes optimal vine canopy development.

## Flowering and Berry Set Limits
Grapevines require moderate thermal conditions during bloom and berry set.
- Daytime temperature threshold: Temperatures exceeding 35°C (95°F) during flowering impair pollen germination and cause severe berry drop (coulure).
- Extreme heat exposure: Temperatures above 38°C (100°F) halt photosynthesis and lead to berry sunburn and shriveling.
- Nighttime limit: Night temperatures above 25°C (77°F) degrade anthocyanin synthesis and reduce color formation in red grape varieties.

## Water Stress and Soil Moisture Limits
- Soil wetness threshold: Root-zone soil wetness index should be maintained above 0.30 during flowering and early berry development.
