# Texas A&M AgriLife Extension: Tomato Planting & Germination Guide
Source: Texas A&M AgriLife Extension Service (EHT-043: "Texas Tomato Growing Guide")
URL: https://agrilifelearn.tamu.edu/s/
Crop: Tomato
Category: Planting, Temperature, Germination, Stage: Planting

## Spring Planting Windows
Spring planting is dictated by soil temperature at transplant depth.
- Soil temperature threshold: Tomato transplants require a minimum soil temperature of 15.5°C (60°F) at a 10 cm (4-inch) depth to establish root systems.
- Air temperature threshold: Planting should begin only when night air temperatures are consistently above 10°C (50°F).
- Risk of cold: Exposed transplants face stunted growth or death if air temperatures drop below 4.4°C (40°F).

## Fall Planting Windows
Fall tomato planting requires managing high-heat thresholds for young transplants.
- Establishment challenge: Transplants are typically set in the field during July and August when temperatures frequently exceed 35°C (95°F).
- Heat protection: Root-zone moisture must be kept consistently high (GWETROOT above 0.5) to cool the root zone. Shade cloths are recommended if daytime temperatures exceed 37.8°C (100°F).
- Frost deadline: Fall varieties must be selected to mature at least 2 weeks before the average first frost date (0°C / 32°F).
