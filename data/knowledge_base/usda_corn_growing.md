# USDA Extension: Corn Growth & Temperature Stress Guide
Source: USDA Agricultural Research Service & Iowa State Extension (PM 1563)
URL: https://store.extension.iastate.edu/product/1563
Crop: Corn
Category: Temperature, Heat Stress, Flowering, Irrigation

## Planting and Germination Limits
Corn seed germination requires specific soil temperature thresholds.
- Minimum soil temperature threshold: 10°C (50°F). Germination below 10°C is severely delayed and vulnerable to seed rot.
- Optimum soil temperature range: 18°C to 30°C (65°F to 86°F).
- Maximum threshold for planting: Transplants or seed establishment faces severe heat stress if soil temperature exceeds 35°C (95°F).

## Flowering and Silking Heat Limits
Corn pollination (silking stage) is extremely sensitive to ambient heat stress.
- Daytime temperature threshold: Daytime temperatures exceeding 35°C (95°F) during flowering cause rapid desiccation of silk and loss of pollen viability.
- Extreme heat limit: Temperatures above 38°C (100°F) during silking cause severe kernel abortion and yield reduction.
- Nighttime limit: Night temperatures exceeding 24°C (75°F) increase maintenance respiration and reduce grain filling.

## Irrigation and Moisture Requirements
- Moisture threshold: Root-zone soil wetness should be maintained above 0.35 during pollination to prevent stress-induced kernel abortion.
